# Code of conduct

TFS Ripast contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and harassment-free.

Be considerate, disagree with ideas rather than people, accept responsibility for mistakes, and keep technical discussion focused on improving the project. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct is harmful to the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
